**Public portfolio summary · Updated October 17, 2026**

[Infin8 Apps](https://infin8apps.com/) · [Infin8 Arcade](https://infin8apps.com/games/) · [FlowMyGosh](https://flowmygosh.infin8apps.com/) · [GitHub profile](https://github.com/JacoInfin8)

## Overview

JacoInfin8 develops Infin8 Apps: an interconnected portfolio of local-first
AI and Windows tools, audio production systems, creator software, browser games,
support workflows and hosted web services. The public website presents products
and access options; individual product demonstrations and technical review can
be arranged for collaborators, creators, employers and prospective partners.

The products share a design principle: understandable interfaces, direct user
control, safe automation, and verified evidence before consequential actions.

## Music-video creation at FlowMyGosh

FlowMyGosh brings creator-owned audio, lyrics and imagery into a hosted editing
workspace with animated lyric treatments, scene positioning, previews and render
workflows. Projects are maintained independently of Infin8 Windows licensing.
Private processing resources are used for GPU workloads. Mobile lyric positioning
and long-form prepared-composition previews are active QA areas; test renders
and production throughput must be checked with the live Studio Host.

**Collaboration direction:** musician/creator demonstrations, workflow feedback,
music-video concepts, visual campaigns and clearly scoped partnership work.

## Engineering capabilities

- **Local AI and operations:** Rust/Tauri, React, TypeScript, Python, SQLite,
  speech/voice integrations, local model routing and controlled tool execution.
- **Web and cloud:** Cloudflare Workers, assets, D1, Durable Objects where needed,
  private service integration, signed interaction endpoints and resource budgeting.
- **Creator media:** editing timelines, lyric compositing, Remotion, image/video
  model workflows, protected source media, GPU-aware rendering and preview UX.
- **Audio:** realtime C++ signal processing, iPlug2, MIDI, standalone Windows
  workflows, host tempo, audio device management and VST3 packaging.
- **Games:** browser 2D/3D, shared controls, mobile input, physics prototypes,
  progression, profiles, and verified-result architecture.

## Public-release and evidence policy

This report distinguishes **currently accessible web pages**, **implemented
software source**, **controlled early access**, and **future or unverified
functionality**. A deployed page does not prove an entire multiplayer, GPU
rendering, paid checkout or native hardware workflow is ready.

Public purchasing and general trials for Infin8 Audio/Windows apps are not
currently open. The internal Assistant is not a customer-facing release.
Features, free limits and access terms should always be confirmed on the
product's own live website or through a current demonstration.
