## Mandatory Infin8 design-system contract

Before changing, building, packaging, capturing, or publishing customer-facing UI, read docs/INFIN8-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md in full and apply the public-portfolio profile plus all repository-specific rules. The contract applies to every agent, terminal, model, and provider. UI frameworks, themes, component libraries, designers, and runtimes must be free to use for commercial Infin8 releases: no purchase, subscription, royalty, paid commercial license, trial, watermark, revenue cap, or paid feature gate. Approved open-source dependencies must still satisfy their license obligations and must not replace the Infin8 visual identity.

Do not refresh product imagery until the intended UI and core workflows have been validated. Do not create ZIPs, installers, portable packages, release directories, or other distribution artifacts unless the user explicitly requests a release and confirms the product is finished. Preserve existing repository build, release, security, accessibility, and product-specific instructions.
